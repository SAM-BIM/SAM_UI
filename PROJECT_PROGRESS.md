- **Root cause:** product defect in `ThermalSourceEntry`. `ThermalSourceCatalog.LoadAsync` reported progress through a `Progress<string>` whose callback did `if (State == Loading) Message = x;`, while the outcome was published in separate unlocked steps (source, message, state). Public behaviour, texts and API unchanged (only the `internal` setters of `State`/`Message` and `SetSource` removed; no other users).
- **Decisions:** the flaky test was not weakened (no sleep, no looser assertion); a new deterministic regression test (queueing `SynchronizationContext` delivering held reports at each notification) pins the outcome publication. The shared check+write lock in `Report` is covered by stress and reasoning, not by a dedicated test (it needs a real thread interleaving).
